# Pilot terms

**This is a draft.** It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

RCOS Compass is in a pilot. These terms say what that means, in the words we
would want to read if somebody offered us a tool for our own governance.

## What a pilot means

- **It will break.** You are among the first communities using this, and you
  will find things that are wrong. Tell us — there is a report button on every
  screen, and what you write there becomes work rather than a chat message.
- **There is no service level.** We run this instance carefully, we take
  backups, and we have practised restoring them. We do not promise uptime.
- **Nothing is charged during the pilot.**

## Your data

- **It is yours.** Export it whenever you like, in formats that open without us.
  Every community also has a git repository of its own history.
- **We do not use it.** Your rules, decisions and discussions are not read by us,
  not mined, and not used to train anything.
- **Ask and it is deleted.** A community can ask for everything of its own to be
  deleted, and a person can erase themselves from their account screen. What that
  does and does not reach is in the privacy policy, stated plainly.
- **Hosting is in Germany**, with backups in the EU, and the third parties
  involved are listed in the sub-processor list.

## What we ask of you

- Tell us when something is wrong, rather than working around it.
- Do not put anything in here that would be catastrophic to lose. We have
  backups and we have tested them, and this is still a pilot.
- Governance content is your community's responsibility. We do not moderate it,
  and we are not a party to your decisions.

## What is not settled yet

- **There is no data processing agreement.** For a pilot with communities we
  know, we have not put one in place, and that is a deliberate gap rather than an
  oversight. Before general availability there will be one, with a legal entity
  behind it.
- **Terms of service and a DPA come with that entity**, not with this document.

## Ending it

Either of us can stop at any time. If we stop, you get an export and a copy of
your repository first, and a month's notice unless you ask us to be quicker.
