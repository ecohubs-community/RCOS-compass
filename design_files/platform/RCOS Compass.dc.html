Layer 1 · Membership/ Membership Charter/ satisfies 1.2.5 · 1.2.6 · 1.2.7 MUST
Version history Export
The requirement
RCOS-CORE 1.2.5
“A community MUST define the conditions under which a person who has been admitted is not yet a full member, including the maximum duration of that state, the rights withheld during it, and the act by which it ends.”
RCOS-CORE 1.2.6
“The ending of a probationary state MUST be a recorded decision, not a lapse of time alone.”
Why it matters
Most disputes about belonging start here: someone believed they were in, the group believed they were still being watched. Naming the end of the trial removes the ambiguity that later becomes a conflict case.
What NOT to define here
Related clauses in other layers: 2.1.3 (who admits), 4.2.1 (disputed exits).
What we said v2 · adopted 2026-06-01 superseded draft v3 in discussion
A person admitted by the Welcome Circle holds candidate status for six months from the date of their arrival. Candidates take part in all discussions and hold no blocking right in consent decisions. Candidates who attend the assembly regularly and complete their contribution hours become full members by a consent decision of the assembly at the end of the six months. The assembly may extend candidacy once, by three months, with reasons recorded.
In plain words
You are a candidate for your first six months. You can speak in everything and block nothing. At six months the assembly decides out loud whether you are a member — it does not happen automatically. If people need more time, they can add three months once, and they have to say why.

Article IV — Membership

4.1  New residents are admitted to the association by vote of the council at any ordinary meeting, provided that notice of the application has been posted for fourteen days.

4.2  Any member wishing to depart shall give the council no less than sixty days written notice, and shall be repaid their capital share within one year of departure. The council may shorten this period at its discretion.

4.3  Members shall contribute to the upkeep of the common house and grounds according to their ability, and shall attend the ordinary meetings of the association.

4.4  A member who fails without excuse to attend three consecutive ordinary meetings may be asked by the council to account for their absence.

4.5  New residents are admitted on a trial basis for a period to be determined by the council, not exceeding one year.

4.6  The association may, by two-thirds vote of the members present, terminate the membership of any person for conduct injurious to the association or its purposes.

Proposal · v3 by Marco Silva, 19 Aug · 3 support · 1 objection
A member may leave at any time by telling the assembly in writing. Within three months of that notice, the treasurer and one other member settle what is owed in both directions: capital share, outstanding contribution hours, and any community objects held privately. Objects bought with common money stay with the community. The settlement is recorded in the decision register, and the leaving member signs it.
